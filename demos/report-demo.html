BEFORE

Ten sources, and no single view of a chapter

To prepare for one chapter visit, a regional leader had to open all ten of these. Each had its own login, its own refresh date and its own way of naming chapters.

Ten logins, ten datesEvery source refreshed on its own schedule, so the numbers never described the same moment.
Three names for one chapterAccount names, codes and city names had to be matched by hand before anything could be compared.
No view of who needed help firstNothing ranked chapters by need, so visits were planned on instinct rather than evidence.
AFTER

One report, ten lenses, ranked by who needs support first

All ten sources, matched to one chapter name and refreshed on a fixed cadence. Click any chapter to open its full profile.

Sorted by concern, highest first
Concern score: 4 per open commitment, plus half of (100 minus health score), plus 0.3 x (100 minus satisfaction), plus 10 if engagement is under 60%, plus 3 per bylaws gap.
