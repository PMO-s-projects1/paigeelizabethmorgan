Four logins, four dates

Each source refreshed on its own schedule, so the numbers never described the same moment.

Three names for one chapter

Account names, codes and city names had to be matched by hand before anything could be compared.

No ranking of concern

Nothing showed which chapters needed attention first, so visits were planned on instinct.

After: one report, five lenses, ranked by concern

Every source is matched to one chapter name and refreshed on a fixed cadence. The Commitments tab is a separate live report, embedded inside this one.

As of
Concern score = 4 per unmet commitment + half of (100 minus health score) + 0.3 x (100 minus satisfaction) + 10 if engagement is under 60% + 3 per bylaws gap. Highest first.
