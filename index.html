My AI strategy and 90-day plan

Many organisations hand out licences, run a prompt workshop and see little change. I embed AI into everyday work, safely and effectively, by building AI fluency across the workplace.

From a tool to a collaborator

People work with AI in three ways. A good strategy moves them through all three, at a safe pace.

AI as a tool

Automation

AI carries out a specific task, following your instructions.

AI as a thinking partner

Augmentation

You and AI work together, each bringing what the other can't.

AI as a collaborator

Agency

AI carries out future tasks on your behalf, within limits you define.

The three modes and the four Ds I teach come from the AI Fluency Framework by Rick Dakan, Joseph Feller and Anthropic, shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

The first 90 days

Days 1 to 30

Listen and baseline

Understand how work really gets done, and agree what success looks like.

The steps
  1. A listening tour with leaders, IT, legal and the people doing the work
  2. Use-case discovery with three teams, every idea scored
  3. Plain-language guardrails agreed with IT and legal
  4. One quick win for a willing team

Measured byBaseline captured, three teams engaged, sponsor sign-off.

Days 31 to 60

Pilot and teach

Prove value on a few workflows, and build the people who will spread it.

The steps
  1. Two or three pilots, each with an owner and a success measure
  2. Role-based learning: short tutorials and live workshops
  3. A champions network, trained through train-the-trainer

Measured byActivation in pilot teams, session ratings, champions trained.

Days 61 to 90

Prove and scale

Show the impact in numbers, and make the new way of working the default.

The steps
  1. Pilots measured against the baseline
  2. Winning workflows scaled to the next teams
  3. AI built into onboarding, with a governance rhythm
  4. A 90-day readout and a two-quarter roadmap

Measured byWeekly active use, hours saved, confidence, workflows in daily use.

Beyond 90 days: two academies

One academy for everyone, and one for the Champions who keep it growing.

Curious to Confident

AI Academy

Teaching every team to use AI in their daily work.

  1. CuriousA safe first try, on their own task.
  2. CapablePractice in how to ask, and how to check.
  3. ConfidentAI built into one real workflow.
Confident to Champion

AI Champion Academy

AI Champions in every department, training their teams.

  1. ConfidentNominated by their department.
  2. ChampionTrain-the-trainer on the four Ds.
  3. In practiceOffice hours, shared workflows and new use cases.
